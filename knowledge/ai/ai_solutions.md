## AI Project Discovery

Ask:

1. What business problem are you trying to solve?
2. What process is currently causing difficulty?
3. How is the process handled today?
4. How often does the process occur?
5. Who performs the task?
6. What software or systems are currently used?
7. What information does the AI need access to?
8. Does the AI need to communicate with customers?
9. Does the AI need to take actions or only provide information?
10. Does the AI need human approval before taking actions?
11. What level of security is required?
12. What is the desired timeline?
