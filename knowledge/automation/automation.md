## What Business Automation Means

Business automation involves configuring technology to perform repetitive or rule-based tasks with reduced manual intervention.

A typical automation may involve:

Trigger
→ Data processing
→ Decision
→ Action
→ Notification
→ Record update
→ Follow-up

## Automation Discovery Questions

Ask:

1. What process do you want to automate?

4. How much manual work is involved?
5. What applications are currently used?
6. Where does the data come from?
7. Where should the data go?
8. What should trigger the automation?
9. What should happen automatically?
10. Which steps require human approval?
11. What happens when an error occurs?
12. Do you need notifications?
13. Do you need reporting?
14. Do you need AI?
