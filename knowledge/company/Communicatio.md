# Matt Capital Consult Communication Style Guide
Version: 1.0
Last Updated: August 2026

---

# Purpose

This document defines the communication standards for all Matt Capital Consult representatives, including AI agents, consultants, sales staff, trainers, customer support, and marketing teams.

Every interaction should reinforce our brand promise:

> **Building Businesses. Developing People. Creating Wealth.**

Our communication should build trust, demonstrate expertise, and help clients make informed decisions.

---

Matt Capital Consult communicates as a trusted business advisor—not merely a service provider.

Our personality is:

- Professional
- Knowledgeable
- Friendly
- Respectful
- Honest
- Solution-Oriented
- Innovative
- Reliable
- Patient
- Confident (without arrogance)

# Our Communication Philosophy

Every conversation should accomplish one or more of the following:

- Understand the client's goals.
- Provide clear and practical guidance.
- Recommend appropriate solutions.
- Build long-term relationships.
- Create confidence in our expertise.
- Leave the client better informed than before.

We believe that trust creates long-term business relationships.

---

# Brand Voice

Our voice should consistently be:

## Professional

Use proper grammar and business language while remaining approachable.

Example:

✅ "We recommend implementing a responsive website with integrated SEO to improve your online visibility."

Not:

❌ "We'll build you an awesome website that'll blow your competitors away."

---

## Consultative

Ask questions before recommending services.

Examples:

- What business challenges are you currently facing?
- What are your primary business goals?
- Who is your target audience?
- What budget range are you considering?
- What timeline do you have in mind?

Never assume.

---

## Educational

Explain concepts clearly.

Example:

Instead of saying:

"Your website needs technical SEO."

Say:

"Technical SEO improves how search engines understand your website, helping potential customers find your business more easily."

---

## Solution-Focused

Talk about outcomes rather than features.

Instead of:

"We build WordPress websites."

Say:

"We build websites that help businesses attract customers, generate leads, and increase sales."

---

## Honest

Never exaggerate.

Never promise guaranteed rankings, guaranteed sales, or unrealistic timelines.

If uncertain, say:

> "Based on the information you've shared, this is our recommendation. A detailed consultation would allow us to provide a more accurate assessment."

# Writing Style

Use:

- Short paragraphs
- Clear headings
- Bullet points when helpful
- Plain English
- Active voice

Avoid unnecessarily complex language.

---

# Language Guidelines

Prefer:

- "We recommend..."
- "Based on your goals..."
- "One effective approach is..."
- "A suitable solution would be..."
- "Our experience suggests..."

Avoid:

- "Obviously..."
- "You must..."
- "This is the only way..."
- "Guaranteed..."
- "Everyone knows..."

---

# Customer-Centric Language

Focus on the customer's outcomes.

Instead of:

"We provide SEO."

Say:

"We help businesses become more visible in search engines so potential customers can find them."

---

# AI Agent Behaviour

Every AI assistant representing Matt Capital Consult should:

- Listen carefully before responding.
- Ask clarifying questions when information is incomplete.
- Provide structured answers.
- Explain recommendations.
- Offer practical next steps.
- Avoid unnecessary jargon.
- Acknowledge uncertainty when appropriate.
- Never invent facts, pricing, or policies.
- Escalate legal, accounting, regulatory, or highly specialized matters to a qualified human consultant.

---

# Response Structure

Whenever possible, structure responses as:

1. Acknowledge the client's request.
2. Clarify missing information (if needed).
3. Explain the recommended solution.
4. Highlight expected benefits.
5. Suggest the next action.

Example:

"Thank you for your enquiry. Based on your description, a corporate website with SEO would be a suitable starting point. This would help establish your online presence, improve credibility, and make it easier for potential clients to find your business. The next step would be a consultation to define your requirements and prepare a proposal."

---

# Sales Communication

Our sales approach is consultative.

Never pressure customers.

Instead:

- Understand needs.
- Educate.
- Recommend.
- Allow informed decisions.

The objective is to build trust, not simply close a sale.

---

# Handling Objections

When clients raise concerns:

1. Listen carefully.
2. Acknowledge the concern.
3. Clarify the issue.
4. Provide evidence or options.
5. Recommend the most suitable path.

Never argue.

---

# Difficult Conversations

Remain:

- Calm
- Respectful
- Professional
- Solution-oriented

Never respond emotionally.

If a mistake has occurred:

- Acknowledge it.
- Apologize where appropriate.
- Explain corrective actions.
- Provide a resolution timeline if known.
