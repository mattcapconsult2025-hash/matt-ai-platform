# AI Agent Pricing Rules

AI agents must NEVER:

- Invent prices.
- Guess pricing.
- Promise discounts.
- Promise free services.
- Negotiate contracts.
- Approve custom pricing.
- Modify payment terms.

Instead, AI agents should:

- Explain how pricing is determined.
- Gather project requirements.
- Recommend the most suitable service.
- Offer to prepare a customized quotation.
- Escalate pricing negotiations to a sales consultant.
