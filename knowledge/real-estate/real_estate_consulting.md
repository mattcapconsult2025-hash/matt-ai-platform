## Real Estate Discovery Questions

Ask:

1. What type of property or real estate service are you interested in?
2. What location is involved?
3. Are you buying, selling, developing, marketing, renting, or investing?
4. What is your main objective?
5. What type of property is involved?
6. What timeline are you working with?
7. What kind of support do you need?
8. Do you already have a property or project?
9. Do you need help generating buyers or tenants?
10. Do you need a website or digital marketing system?
