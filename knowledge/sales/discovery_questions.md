# Matt Capital Consult – Discovery Questions Guide
Version: 1.0
Last Updated: August 2026

---

# Purpose

This document provides the official discovery framework used by Matt Capital Consult.

Its purpose is to help AI agents and consultants:

- Understand the client's business.
- Identify business challenges.
- Recommend appropriate services.
- Prepare accurate proposals.
- Build long-term client relationships.

Discovery should always come before recommendations.

---

# Discovery Principles

Every discovery conversation should:

- Be conversational, not an interrogation.
- Focus on understanding the client.
- Ask relevant questions only.
- Avoid unnecessary repetition.
- Respect the client's time.
- Adapt to the client's level of knowledge.
- Gather enough information to make informed recommendations.

---

# General Discovery Process

Every client conversation should follow these stages:

1. Introduction
2. Business Understanding
3. Challenge Identification
4. Goal Definition
5. Current Situation Assessment
6. Project Scope
7. Timeline
8. Budget
9. Decision Process
10. Next Steps

---

# Stage 1 – Introduction

## Objectives

Build rapport and understand why the client contacted Matt Capital Consult.

### Questions

- What can we help you achieve today?
- Could you briefly describe your business?
- Which service are you interested in?
- How did you hear about Matt Capital Consult?

---

# Stage 2 – Business Understanding

Understand the organization.

### Questions

- What products or services do you provide?
- Who are your primary customers?
- How long have you been operating?
- What industry do you serve?
- How large is your organization?
- What makes your business unique?

---

# Stage 3 – Business Challenges

Identify the real problem.

### Questions

- What challenge are you trying to solve?
- What prompted you to seek assistance now?
- How is this challenge affecting your business?
- What have you already tried?
- What has worked well?
- What has not worked?

---

# Stage 4 – Business Goals

Understand the desired outcome.

### Questions

- What would success look like?
- What are your short-term objectives?
- What are your long-term objectives?
- How will you measure success?
- Which outcome is most important?

---

# Stage 5 – Current Situation

Assess the client's existing environment.

### Questions

- What systems or tools do you currently use?
- Do you already have a website?
- Are you currently using AI?
- Do you have internal processes for this area?
- Are you working with another provider?

---

# Stage 6 – Project Scope

Clarify expectations.

### Questions

- What would you like us to deliver?
- Are there any required features?
- Is this a new project or an upgrade?
- Are there any technical requirements?
- Are there any regulatory requirements?

---

# Stage 7 – Timeline

Understand urgency.

### Questions

- When would you like the project to begin?
- Is there a deadline?
- Are there important milestones?
- Is this linked to a business event or launch?

---

# Stage 8 – Budget

Discuss budget professionally.

### Questions

- Do you have an estimated budget range?
- Has funding already been approved?
- Are you evaluating multiple options?

Budget questions should help recommend suitable solutions—not disqualify the client.

---

# Stage 9 – Decision Making

Identify stakeholders.

### Questions

- Who will make the final decision?
- Who else will be involved?
- What is your approval process?
- Are there procurement requirements?

---

# Stage 10 – Next Steps

Confirm expectations.

### Questions

- Would you like a proposal?
- Would you prefer a consultation?
- Is a demonstration helpful?
- When would you like us to follow up?

# Qualification Indicators

Positive indicators include:

- Clear business objectives
- Defined project scope
- Decision-maker involvement
- Real business need
- Realistic timeline
- Budget awareness
- Willingness to collaborate

---

# Warning Indicators

Proceed carefully when:

- Objectives are unclear.
- Requirements change frequently.
- Decision-makers are unavailable.
- Expectations are unrealistic.
- The requested timeline is impractical.
- Required information is withheld.

These indicators should prompt additional clarification rather than immediate rejection.

---

# AI Agent Behaviour

AI agents should:

- Ask one topic at a time where practical.
- Adapt questions based on previous answers.
- Avoid repeating questions already answered.
- Summarize key information periodically.
- Confirm understanding before making recommendations.
- Escalate complex or regulated matters to a human consultant.

AI agents should not:

- Assume facts.
- Rush to recommend services.
- Overwhelm the client with too many questions.
- Pressure the client for immediate decisions.
