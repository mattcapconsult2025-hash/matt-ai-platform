# Matt Capital Consult – Lead Qualification Framework
Version: 1.0
Last Updated: August 2026

---

# Purpose

This document defines the official lead qualification framework used by Matt Capital Consult.

It helps AI agents and consultants:

- Understand client needs
- Identify business opportunities
- Recommend appropriate services
- Prioritize sales opportunities
- Improve proposal quality
- Increase conversion rates

The objective is to understand the client's goals before recommending solutions.

---

# Qualification Principles

Every interaction should:

- Focus on solving business problems.
- Ask relevant discovery questions.
- Avoid assumptions.
- Respect the client's time.
- Recommend only appropriate services.
- Build trust through professional advice.

The goal is to become a trusted advisor rather than simply a service provider.

---

# Qualification Process

The qualification process consists of six stages:

1. Initial Contact
2. Business Discovery
3. Needs Assessment
4. Solution Mapping
5. Opportunity Qualification
6. Proposal & Next Steps

---

# Stage 1 – Initial Contact

## Objectives

- Welcome the client.
- Understand why they contacted Matt Capital Consult.
- Identify the service category.
- Determine whether immediate assistance is possible.

## Information to Collect

- Name
- Company name
- Role
- Industry
- Preferred contact method
- Location (if relevant)

---

# Stage 2 – Business Discovery

Understand the client's business.

Questions include:

- What does your business do?
- Who are your customers?
- How long have you been operating?
- What products or services do you offer?
- What are your current business priorities?
- What challenges are you experiencing?

Do not ask unnecessary questions if the client has already provided the information.

---

# Stage 3 – Needs Assessment

Determine the underlying problem.

Explore:

- Desired outcomes
- Current challenges
- Existing solutions
- Success criteria
- Urgency
- Internal resources

Examples:

- What would success look like?
- What prompted you to seek help now?
- What have you already tried?
- What is preventing you from achieving your goal?

Focus on the business problem, not just the requested service.

---

# Stage 4 – Solution Mapping

Match client needs with Matt Capital Consult services.

Examples:

| Client Need | Recommended Services |
|-------------|----------------------|
| Start a business | Business Consulting |
| Improve online presence | Website Design |
| Increase sales | Digital Marketing |
| Automate repetitive work | AI Solutions |
| Train employees | Training Academy |
| Improve reporting | Data Analytics |
| Develop a property project | Real Estate Consulting |

Only recommend services that address the identified need.

---

# Stage 5 – Opportunity Qualification

Evaluate whether the opportunity is suitable.

Consider:

- Business need
- Budget expectations
- Decision-making authority
- Timeline
- Project readiness
- Technical feasibility

This stage helps prioritize follow-up activities.

---

# Stage 6 – Proposal & Next Steps

Where appropriate:

- Summarize the client's objectives.
- Recommend the proposed solution.
- Explain the expected outcomes.
- Outline the next steps.
- Arrange a consultation or proposal.

# Discovery Framework (BANT+)

Matt Capital Consult uses an enhanced qualification framework based on BANT.

## B – Budget

Understand whether funding is available.

Examples:

- Do you have an estimated budget range?
- Has funding been approved?
- Is this part of an existing project?

Budget discussions should help recommend suitable solutions—not exclude potential clients.

---

## A – Authority

Identify decision-makers.

Questions:

- Who will make the final decision?
- Will anyone else be involved in the approval process?

---

## N – Need

Clearly understand the problem.

Examples:

- What challenge are you trying to solve?
- Why is this important?
- What impact is the problem having?

---

## T – Timeline

Determine urgency.

Examples:

- When would you like the project to begin?
- Is there a deadline?
- Are there any important milestones?

---

## + Strategic Fit

Assess alignment with Matt Capital Consult.

Consider:

- Industry
- Business objectives
- Technical requirements
- Long-term relationship potential

# Service-Specific Qualification

## Business Consulting

Ask:

- What type of business do you operate?
- What business challenge are you facing?
- Are you launching, growing, or restructuring?
- Have you worked with consultants before?

---

## Website Development

Ask:

- Do you already have a website?
- What is the website's purpose?
- Do you need online payments?
- Will you manage the content yourself?
- Do you require SEO?

---

## AI Solutions

Ask:

- Which tasks do you want to automate?
- Do you already use AI?
- Which systems should AI integrate with?
- Do you need internal or customer-facing AI?

---

## Digital Marketing

Ask:

- Who is your target audience?
- Which marketing channels do you currently use?
- What are your goals?
- Are you currently running campaigns?

---

## Training Academy

Ask:

- Individual or corporate training?
- Current experience level?
- Preferred learning format?
- Number of participants?
- Certification required?

---

## Real Estate Consulting

Ask:

- What type of property is involved?
- Are you investing, developing, or marketing?
- Which location?
- What stage is the project at?

# Qualification Scorecard

Assign a score for each area.

| Category | Score |
|----------|------:|
| Business Need | 0–5 |
| Strategic Fit | 0–5 |
| Decision Authority | 0–5 |
| Budget Readiness | 0–5 |
| Timeline | 0–5 |
| Technical Feasibility | 0–5 |
| Relationship Potential | 0–5 |

Maximum Score: **35**

---

# Opportunity Rating

| Score | Rating | Recommended Action |
|-------:|--------|--------------------|
| 30–35 | Excellent | Prioritize proposal and consultation |
| 24–29 | High | Proceed with proposal |
| 18–23 | Medium | Continue discovery and nurturing |
| 10–17 | Low | Provide guidance and follow up later |
| 0–9 | Very Low | Offer educational resources or referral if appropriate |

Scores guide prioritization—they do not replace professional judgement.

# AI Agent Behaviour

AI agents should:

- Be professional and courteous.
- Ask one topic at a time where possible.
- Avoid overwhelming the client.
- Summarize information accurately.
- Recommend relevant services only.
- Escalate complex matters to a consultant.

AI agents must not:

- Pressure clients.
- Promise outcomes.
- Invent information.
- Assume budgets.
- Provide legal, tax, or regulated financial advice.

# Handover to Sales Consultant

Escalate when:

- A detailed proposal is required.
- Pricing negotiation begins.
- Contracts are requested.
- Technical architecture is complex.
- The project involves regulatory requirements.
- Executive stakeholders request strategic meetings.

Provide a concise summary of all discovery information collected.
