# Matt Capital Consult — Lead Qualification Framework

## Purpose

The qualification framework helps Matt AI determine whether a prospect is a good opportunity for Matt Capital Consult.

## Qualification Categories

Evaluate:

1. Need
2. Budget
3. Authority
4. Timeline
5. Fit
6. Complexity
7. Intent

## Need

### Strong Need

The prospect has a clear business problem that Matt Capital Consult can solve.

Examples:

- Needs a new website
- Needs an e-commerce platform
- Needs more leads
- Needs automation
- Needs an AI assistant
- Needs digital marketing
- Needs training

### Weak Need

The prospect is only researching without a defined problem.

## Budget

### Strong

The prospect has a realistic budget or is willing to discuss project scope and quotation.

### Unclear

The prospect has not established a budget.

Action:

Continue discovery.

### Poor Fit

The prospect's expectations are clearly inconsistent with the required scope.

Action:

Explain the likely scope and avoid making unrealistic promises.

## Authority

Determine whether the prospect:

- Is the owner
- Is a director
- Is a manager
- Is responsible for the project
- Needs approval from another person

## Timeline

### Urgent

The prospect wants to start soon and has a defined deadline.

### Active

The prospect intends to proceed but has no immediate deadline.

### Future

The prospect is planning for a later period.

## Fit

A prospect is a strong fit when their needs align with Matt Capital Consult's capabilities.

Potential areas include:

- Business consulting
- Human capital development
- Corporate training
- Digital transformation
- Website design
- SEO
- Digital marketing
- AI business solutions
- Business automation
- Real estate consulting

## Complexity

Classify projects as:

### Basic

Can potentially be delivered using standard services/packages.

### Moderate

Requires customization, integrations, or multiple services.

### Complex

Requires:

- Custom software
- Multiple integrations
- Advanced AI
- Large-scale automation
- Enterprise architecture
- Extensive data processing

Complex projects should normally be escalated for human consultation.

## Intent

### High Intent

Prospect asks about:

- Price
- Timeline
- Payment
- Starting
- Consultation
- Proposal
- Quotation

### Medium Intent

Prospect asks detailed questions and compares solutions.

### Low Intent

Prospect asks general questions without showing buying intent.

## Lead Score Guidance

The AI may classify leads qualitatively as:

- Hot
- Warm
- Cold

Do not invent numerical scores unless a specific scoring system has been configured.

## Hot Lead

Strong need + reasonable budget + active timeline + strong intent.

Action:

Move quickly toward consultation or quotation.

## Warm Lead

Clear interest but one or more qualification factors remain uncertain.

Action:

Continue discovery and provide relevant information.

## Cold Lead

Low intent or no immediate need.

Action:

Provide useful information and invite future engagement.

## Escalation

Human review is recommended for:

- Large projects
- Complex AI systems
- Enterprise automation
- Major real estate projects
- Custom software
- Contract negotiations
- Pricing negotiations
- Unusual requirements
- Complaints
- Legal or regulatory questions
