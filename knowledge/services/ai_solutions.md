# Discovery Questions for AI Agents

Before recommending AI services, ask:

1. What type of business do you operate?
2. What business challenge are you trying to solve?
3. Which tasks consume the most time?
4. Do you currently use any AI tools?
5. What software systems do you already use?
6. Do you need customer-facing AI, internal AI, or both?
7. Do you require workflow automation?
8. Do you have existing documentation that an AI assistant could use?
9. How many employees would use the solution?
10. What is your implementation timeline?
11. Do you have an estimated budget?

# Frequently Asked Questions

## Can AI replace my employees?

AI is best used to automate repetitive tasks and assist people. It is most effective when combined with human expertise.

---

## Can AI understand my business?

Yes. AI assistants can be trained using your company's approved knowledge, policies, products, and documentation. The quality of responses depends on the quality and maintenance of the knowledge base.

---

## Can AI work with my existing systems?

Many AI solutions can integrate with existing business systems, subject to technical compatibility and project scope.

---

## Is AI secure?

Security depends on the chosen solution, deployment approach, data handling practices, and governance. We work with clients to recommend appropriate security measures, but organizations remain responsible for their own security and compliance obligations.

---

## Do I need technical knowledge?

No. We provide guidance, implementation support, and training appropriate to the client's level of experience.

# AI Agent Guidelines

When discussing AI solutions:

- Explain AI in clear, practical language.
- Focus on business outcomes rather than technical complexity.
- Avoid overstating AI capabilities.
- Do not claim AI is always accurate or error-free.
- Explain that human oversight remains important.
- Recommend responsible implementation.
- Escalate requests involving regulated advice, cybersecurity, or complex custom integrations to a qualified consultant.
