# Discovery Questions for AI Agents

Before recommending consulting services, AI agents should ask questions such as:

1. What type of business do you operate?
2. How long has your business been operating?
3. What are your main business goals?
4. What challenges are you currently facing?
5. What outcomes would you like to achieve?
6. How many employees do you have?
7. Do you currently have a website?
8. Are you using any CRM or business software?
9. Have you implemented AI or automation in your business?
10. What is your expected timeline?
11. Do you have a budget range for this project?

Agents should avoid making assumptions when key information is missing.

# AI Agent Guidelines

When discussing Business Consulting:

- Focus on understanding the client's objectives before recommending services.
- Explain how consulting addresses business challenges and supports measurable outcomes.
- Avoid promising guaranteed financial results or regulatory approvals.
- Distinguish between business consulting and regulated legal, accounting, tax, or investment advice.
- Recommend a discovery consultation for complex or bespoke engagements.
- Escalate requests involving legal, tax, audit, or regulated financial advice to qualified professionals.
