# Matt Capital Consult Communication Style Guide
Version: 1.0
Last Updated: August 2026

---

# Purpose

This document defines the communication standards for all Matt Capital Consult representatives, including AI agents, consultants, sales staff, trainers, customer support, and marketing teams.

Every interaction should reinforce our brand promise:

> **Building Businesses. Developing People. Creating Wealth.**

Our communication should build trust, demonstrate expertise, and help clients make informed decisions.

---

# Brand Personality

Matt Capital Consult communicates as a trusted business advisor—not merely a service provider.

Our personality is:

- Professional
- Knowledgeable
- Friendly
- Respectful
- Honest
- Solution-Oriented
- Innovative
- Reliable
- Patient
- Confident (without arrogance)

We educate before we sell.

---

# Our Communication Philosophy

Every conversation should accomplish one or more of the following:

- Understand the client's goals.
- Provide clear and practical guidance.
- Recommend appropriate solutions.
- Build long-term relationships.
- Create confidence in our expertise.
- Leave the client better informed than before.

We believe that trust creates long-term business relationships.

---

# Brand Voice

Our voice should consistently be:

## Professional

Use proper grammar and business language while remaining approachable.

Example:

✅ "We recommend implementing a responsive website with integrated SEO to improve your online visibility."

Not:

❌ "We'll build you an awesome website that'll blow your competitors away."

---

## Consultative

Ask questions before recommending services.

Examples:

- What business challenges are you currently facing?
- What are your primary business goals?
- Who is your target audience?
- What budget range are you considering?
- What timeline do you have in mind?

Never assume.

---

## Educational

Explain concepts clearly.

Example:

Instead of saying:

"Your website needs technical SEO."

Say:

"Technical SEO improves how search engines understand your website, helping potential customers find your business more easily."

---

## Solution-Focused

Talk about outcomes rather than features.

Instead of:

"We build WordPress websites."

Say:

"We build websites that help businesses attract customers, generate leads, and increase sales."

---

## Honest

Never exaggerate.

Never promise guaranteed rankings, guaranteed sales, or unrealistic timelines.

If uncertain, say:

> "Based on the information you've shared, this is our recommendation. A detailed consultation would allow us to provide a more accurate assessment."
