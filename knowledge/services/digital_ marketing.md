# Discovery Questions for AI Agents

Before recommending marketing services, ask:

1. What products or services do you offer?
2. Who is your target audience?
3. What are your primary business goals?
4. Which marketing channels are you currently using?
5. Do you already have a website?
6. Are you currently running advertisements?
7. What challenges are you facing?
8. What marketing activities have worked well previously?
9. Do you have a content creation process?
10. What is your estimated marketing budget?
11. What timeline do you have for achieving your goals?

Avoid recommending campaigns without understanding the client's objectives.

# Frequently Asked Questions

## How long before digital marketing produces results?

The timeline varies depending on the marketing channels used, industry competition, campaign quality, and business objectives. Some paid campaigns can generate traffic quickly, while SEO and content marketing generally require a longer-term approach.

---

## Can you guarantee first-page Google rankings?

No. Search engine rankings depend on many factors beyond our control. We implement recognized SEO best practices and continuously optimize campaigns.

---

## Which social media platform is best?

The most suitable platform depends on your audience, industry, products, services, and business goals. We recommend channels based on research rather than assumptions.

---

## Do I need a website before starting digital marketing?

A website is strongly recommended for most campaigns because it serves as the central destination for customers. However, some campaigns can initially focus on other digital assets depending on the business objectives.

---

## How do you measure marketing success?

Success is measured against agreed business objectives and KPIs such as lead generation, conversions, customer engagement, revenue growth, and campaign performance.

# AI Agent Guidelines

When discussing digital marketing:

- Begin by understanding the client's business goals.
- Recommend solutions based on evidence and objectives.
- Explain marketing concepts in clear, non-technical language.
- Avoid promising guaranteed traffic, sales, rankings, or ROI.
- Highlight the importance of continuous testing and optimization.
- Encourage realistic expectations and long-term planning.
- Escalate requests involving highly specialized advertising compliance or legal matters to a qualified consultant.
