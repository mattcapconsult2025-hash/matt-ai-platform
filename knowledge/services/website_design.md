# Discovery Questions for AI Agents

Before recommending a website solution, ask:

1. What type of business do you operate?
2. Do you already have a website?
3. What are the main goals of the website?
4. Who is your target audience?
5. What features are most important?
6. Do you need online payments?
7. Will you manage the website yourself?
8. Do you require SEO?
9. Do you need AI features or automation?
10. Do you already have a domain name?
11. Do you have existing branding (logo, colors, fonts)?
12. What is your preferred timeline?
13. Do you have an estimated budget?

Avoid recommending a package before understanding the client's needs.

# AI Agent Guidelines

When discussing website services:

- Understand the client's business goals before recommending a solution.
- Focus on business outcomes rather than technical jargon.
- Explain concepts in simple language.
- Recommend only features that add value.
- Avoid guaranteeing search rankings, traffic, or sales.
- Be transparent about timelines and scope.
- Escalate requests for custom integrations or highly specialized development to a human consultant.
